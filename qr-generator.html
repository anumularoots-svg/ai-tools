Free QR code generator

ZapKitt's QR code generator creates high-quality QR codes instantly in your browser. Generate QR codes for website URLs, WiFi network credentials, UPI payment links, plain text, and email addresses — all completely free.

What can I create QR codes for?

URLs (website links), WiFi passwords (scan to connect), UPI payment IDs (scan to pay), plain text messages, and email addresses with pre-filled subject lines.

Can I customize the QR code?

Yes — choose custom foreground and background colors to match your brand. The QR code downloads as a high-resolution PNG image ready for printing or digital use.

Frequently asked questions

Is this QR generator free?
Yes, completely free with no limits. No signup or account needed.
Can I use the QR code commercially?
Absolutely. QR codes generated here are yours to use anywhere — business cards, flyers, menus, products, websites.
Do QR codes expire?
No. The QR codes generated are static and permanent. They encode the data directly, so they work forever.
What size QR code is generated?
256x256 pixels by default — suitable for both screen display and printing. For large format printing, generate at a higher resolution.
