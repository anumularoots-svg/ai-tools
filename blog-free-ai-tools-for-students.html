How to Use AI Tools Ethically as a Student

AI tools are like a tutor, not a shortcut. Here's how to use them responsibly:

Do: Use the homework helper to understand solution methods, then solve similar problems yourself. Use the essay writer to get started, then rewrite in your own voice. Use the paraphraser to learn how to express ideas differently.

Don't: Submit AI-generated work as your own without adding your analysis. Copy solutions without understanding the concepts. Ignore your school's academic integrity policy.
