Free AI paraphraser online

ZapKitt's AI paraphraser rewrites your text while keeping the original meaning. Choose from 5 modes: Standard, Formal, Casual, Shorter, and Creative. Perfect for essays, emails, articles, and social media posts.

How does it work?

Our paraphraser uses advanced AI to understand your text and rewrite it in a different way. For best results, connect a free Groq AI API key — it takes 1 minute and is completely free.

Is my text private?

Without an API key, all processing happens in your browser. With an API key, text is sent to Groq's servers for AI processing (Groq's privacy policy applies). Your API key is never stored.
