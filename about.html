Our story

FixPilot was born out of frustration. Every time we needed to compress a PDF or convert a file, we faced the same problems: tools that required signup, added watermarks, limited free usage, or worse — uploaded our private files to unknown servers.

We thought: what if all these tools ran right in your browser? No servers involved, no privacy concerns, no limits. Just tools that work.

Built by a team passionate about web technology and user privacy, FixPilot uses modern browser APIs and AI to deliver powerful tools that respect your data. Every tool on FixPilot processes files locally on your device — your PDFs, images, and documents never leave your browser.

We're constantly adding new tools and improving existing ones. If you have a suggestion or need a tool we don't offer yet, we'd love to hear from you.
