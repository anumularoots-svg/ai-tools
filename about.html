About ZapKitt

We believe everyone deserves access to powerful file tools — without paying, signing up, or compromising their privacy.

Privacy first

Your files never leave your device. While other tools upload your sensitive documents to their servers, we process everything locally in your browser.

Free forever

No premium paywalls, no "3 free uses per day" limits, no watermarks. We believe basic file tools should be accessible to everyone.

Instant results

No upload wait times. Your device handles the processing, which means results in seconds — even on slow internet connections.

Works everywhere

Desktop, mobile, tablet — any modern browser. Works offline after the page loads. Available in 100+ countries worldwide.

Our story

ZapKitt was born out of frustration. Every time we needed to compress a PDF or convert a file, we faced the same problems: tools that required signup, added watermarks, limited free usage, or worse — uploaded our private files to unknown servers.

We thought: what if all these tools ran right in your browser? No servers involved, no privacy concerns, no limits. Just tools that work.

Built by a team passionate about web technology and user privacy, ZapKitt uses modern browser APIs and AI to deliver powerful tools that respect your data. Every tool on ZapKitt processes files locally on your device — your PDFs, images, and documents never leave your browser.

We're constantly adding new tools and improving existing ones. If you have a suggestion or need a tool we don't offer yet, we'd love to hear from you.
