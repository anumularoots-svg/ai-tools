Convert PNG to JPG online for free

ZapKitt converts PNG images to JPG format directly in your browser. PNG files are lossless and often large — converting to JPG with 85% quality typically reduces file size by 60-80% with minimal visible quality loss. Perfect for reducing image size for websites, email attachments, and social media uploads. The background color option handles PNG transparency — transparent areas are filled with your chosen color (default white).

Why convert PNG to JPG?

PNG is lossless and supports transparency, making files larger. JPG uses lossy compression that produces much smaller files. For photos and images without transparency needs, JPG is the better format.

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG doesn't support transparency. ZapKitt fills transparent areas with your chosen background color (default white). You can change this to any color using the color picker.

What quality should I use?

85% is a good balance of quality and file size. For web use, 70-80% is usually fine. For print or high-quality needs, use 90-100%.
