How does ATS optimization work?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software that scans resumes before a human sees them. Our AI extracts the exact keywords, skills, and qualifications from the job description and naturally integrates them into your resume — matching the language the ATS is programmed to look for. This significantly increases your chances of passing the automated screen.

Is this real AI or templates?

This is real AI — Llama 3.3 70B, same class as ChatGPT. Every resume is generated fresh based on your specific experience and the target job description. No pre-written templates, no generic bullet points. Each resume is unique.

Should I use a 1-page or 2-page resume?

If you have 5+ years of experience, use 2 pages. For entry-level to 5 years, 1 page is ideal. Our AI optimizes content density based on your selection — ensuring every line adds value without padding.

Can I edit the generated resume?

Yes. Copy the generated resume and paste it into Google Docs, Word, or any editor. We recommend adding specific numbers/metrics that you know but didn't include in the input — the more specific, the stronger the resume.

Is my data private?

Your information is sent to the AI model for generation but is never stored or logged by FixPilot. Nothing is saved after your session.
