Download as professional PDF — free, no signup, no watermark.

How does ATS optimization work?

ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) scan resumes before humans see them. Our AI extracts the exact keywords, skills, and qualifications from the job description and naturally integrates them into your resume — matching the language the ATS looks for. We run 20 checks including keyword density, formatting compatibility, section structure, and metric usage to ensure maximum pass rates.

What is the STAR framework?

STAR stands for Situation-Task-Action-Result. Instead of generic bullet points like "Managed CI/CD pipelines," AI rewrites them as: "Redesigned multi-stage CI/CD pipelines with parallel security gates (SAST, SCA, container scanning), reducing deployment time by 60% and accelerating release cadence from monthly to weekly." This format proves your impact with specific, measurable results.

Can I paste my existing resume?

Yes! Paste your current resume text and AI will restructure it with better formatting, stronger bullet points, ATS-optimized keywords, and professional summary. Your original experience is preserved — AI enhances the writing, adds metrics, and tailors it to the target job.

Is the PDF really free?

Yes. PDF download is completely free — no watermark, no signup wall, no credit card. We use the Groq free API tier for AI generation and browser-based PDF creation. No server-side processing needed.

1 page, 2 pages, or 3 pages?

Entry level (0-4 years): 1 page. Mid to senior (5-12 years): 2 pages. Very experienced / executive (12+ years): 3 pages. AI optimizes content density based on your selection — ensuring every line adds value without padding or repetition.

Is my data private?

Your information is sent to the AI model for generation but is never stored or logged by ZapKitt. Nothing is saved after your session. The PDF is generated entirely in your browser — your resume never touches our servers.
