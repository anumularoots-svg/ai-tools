Why ATS Scoring Matters in 2026

Over 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before a human ever sees them. The most common reasons are missing keywords, incorrect formatting, and weak bullet points. ZapKitt solves all three.

The ATS scoring system checks four areas: keyword match with the job description (out of 20), skills alignment (out of 20), format compatibility (out of 20), and experience relevance (out of 20). Plus a bonus score for overall quality. Most users achieve 90+ on their first try.

Why STAR Method Beats Generic Bullet Points

Recruiters spend an average of 7.4 seconds scanning each resume. Generic bullet points like "Responsible for managing projects" don't catch attention. STAR-format bullets show measurable impact:

"Managed a cross-functional team of 8 engineers (Situation/Task) to migrate legacy infrastructure to AWS (Action), reducing deployment time by 60% and saving $120K annually (Result)."
