Free online video cutter and trimmer

ZapKitt Video Cutter lets you trim a video clip without uploading it anywhere. Load an MP4, WebM, MOV or OGG file, drag the start and end handles across the timeline to select the exact section you want to keep, preview it, and download the trimmed result as a WebM video. The cut happens locally in your browser by re-recording the selected segment, so your video never leaves your device and there is no watermark. Ideal for grabbing a highlight, removing dead time at the start or end, or shortening a clip for social media.
