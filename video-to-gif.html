Free online video to GIF converter

ZapKitt Video to GIF turns short video clips into shareable animated GIFs entirely in your browser. Load an MP4, WebM, MOV or OGG file, set the start point and length, and choose the output width and frame rate to balance quality against file size. The GIF is rendered locally on your device using canvas and an in-browser GIF encoder — nothing is uploaded to any server. It is perfect for reaction GIFs, product demos, tutorials, and social media clips.
