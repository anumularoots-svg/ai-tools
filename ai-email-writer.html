Is this real AI or just templates?

This is real AI. ZapKitt uses the Llama 3.3 70B model (via Groq) — the same class of AI as ChatGPT. Every email is generated fresh based on your specific context. No pre-written templates.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no daily limits, no premium tier. The AI runs on Groq's free API tier which provides fast, high-quality responses.

Are my emails private?

Your email context is sent to the AI model for generation but is not stored or logged by ZapKitt. The AI processes your request and returns the result — nothing is saved.

Can I edit the generated email?

Absolutely. The generated email is fully editable. We recommend adding your personal touch — specific details, your name, and any company-specific information — before sending.
