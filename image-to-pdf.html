How to convert images to PDF online

ZapKitt converts your images to PDF entirely in your browser using the jsPDF library. Select one or multiple images, arrange them in your preferred order, choose page size and orientation, then click convert. Each image becomes a page in the output PDF. Your images never leave your device — no server upload is required.

Can I combine multiple images into one PDF?

Yes! Select multiple images at once or add them one at a time. Each image becomes a separate page in the PDF. You can reorder pages using the up/down arrows before converting.

What page sizes are supported?

Choose from A4, Letter, A3, or "Fit to Image" which automatically sizes each page to match the image dimensions. You can also set orientation (portrait/landscape) and margins.

What image formats can I convert?

JPEG, PNG, and WebP images are supported. There is no limit on the number of images or file size. All processing happens locally in your browser.
