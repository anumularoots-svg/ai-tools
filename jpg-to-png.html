Convert JPG to PNG online for free

ZapKitt converts JPG images to PNG format in your browser. PNG is a lossless format — converting from JPG to PNG preserves all current quality without any further degradation. PNG also supports transparency, making it ideal for logos, icons, and graphics that need clean edges on any background.

Does JPG to PNG improve quality?

Converting JPG to PNG prevents further quality loss but cannot restore quality already lost in the original JPG compression. The conversion preserves the current state in a lossless format.

Will the file size increase?

Yes, PNG files are typically larger than JPG because PNG is lossless. This is normal and expected. The benefit is no further quality degradation when editing or saving the file.
