Questions

Do I have to pay to use ZapKitt?

No. Every tool works free, every day, with no account and no card. Pro removes the daily ceiling — it does not unlock the actual quality of the results, which is the same either way.

What happens when I hit a limit?

That tool becomes available again the next day. Nothing you have already generated is taken away, and the other tools keep working.

Why is there no free trial?

Because the free tier is the trial, and it does not expire. A trial that turns into a bill is a worse deal than three checks a day forever.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, with no minimum term and no cancellation fee. Your account stays and simply returns to the free limits.

Who processes the payment?

Dodo Payments, acting as merchant of record. They handle the card, the receipt and the tax. ZapKitt never sees your card details.
