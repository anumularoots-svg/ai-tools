Golden Prompt — How It Works

Professional AI image prompt engineers use 15 specific layers when writing prompts — subject identity, pose, expression, clothing, accessories, hair/skin, background, time of day, lighting setup, camera lens, composition, color palette, art style, quality tags, and negative prompts. Most people skip 10 of these layers, which is why their images look generic.

ZapKitt's Golden Prompt AI automatically generates all 15 layers from your simple description. You type "a woman in an office" — the AI creates a full cinematographer-quality prompt with specific lighting direction, lens focal length, color palette, and composition rules.
