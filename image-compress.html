How to compress images online

FixPilot's image compressor reduces JPEG, PNG, and WebP file sizes by up to 80% without visible quality loss. Everything processes in your browser — your images never leave your device.

Why compress images?

Large images slow down websites, take storage space, and are hard to share. Compressed images load faster, use less bandwidth, and are easier to email or upload to social media.
