How to compress a PDF file

ZapKitt's free PDF compressor works entirely in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server — the most private way to reduce your PDF size. Drag and drop your file and get results instantly.

Why compress PDF files?

Large PDFs are hard to share via email (most providers limit attachments to 10-25MB). Compressed PDFs load faster on websites and take less storage. ZapKitt reduces size while keeping quality.

Is it safe to use?

Absolutely. Unlike iLovePDF or SmallPDF that upload files to their servers, ZapKitt processes everything locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device — guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I compress a PDF?
Typically 30-70% reduction. PDFs with images see the most reduction. Text-heavy PDFs may see smaller reductions since text is already compact.
Is there a file size limit?
You can compress PDFs up to 50MB. Since processing happens in your browser, larger files may take a few seconds longer.
Do I need to create an account?
No. ZapKitt is completely free with no signup required. Just drop your file and download the result.
What browsers are supported?
All modern browsers — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — on desktop and mobile devices.
