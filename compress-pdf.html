How to compress a PDF file online

Our free PDF compressor works entirely in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server, making it the most private way to reduce your PDF size. Simply drag and drop your PDF file, and the tool will compress it instantly.

Why compress PDF files?

Large PDF files are difficult to share via email (most email providers limit attachments to 10-25MB). Compressed PDFs load faster on websites and take less storage space. Our tool reduces PDF size while maintaining readability and quality.

Is it safe to compress PDF online?

Yes. Unlike other online tools that upload your files to their servers, our PDF compressor processes everything locally in your web browser. Your files never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

How much can I compress a PDF?

Typical compression reduces file size by 30-70%, depending on the content. PDFs with many images see the most reduction. Text-heavy PDFs may see smaller reductions since text is already compact.
