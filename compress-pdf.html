How to compress a PDF file online

Our free PDF compressor works entirely in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server, making it the most private way to reduce your PDF size. Simply drag and drop your PDF file, and the tool will compress it instantly.

Why compress PDF files?

Large PDF files are difficult to share via email — most providers limit attachments to 10-25MB. Compressed PDFs load faster on websites and take less storage space. Our tool reduces file size while maintaining readability.

Is it safe?

Yes. Unlike other tools that upload files to servers, our compressor processes everything locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy.

How much compression?

Typical compression reduces file size by 30-70%, depending on content. PDFs with many images see the most reduction. Text-heavy PDFs may see smaller reductions since text is already compact.
