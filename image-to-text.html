How to extract text from images

ZapKitt's OCR tool uses AI to recognize and extract text from any image. Upload a photo, screenshot, or scanned document and get editable text instantly. Supports English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, and 100+ other languages.

What is OCR?

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is a technology that converts images of text into actual editable text. It works on photos of documents, screenshots, handwritten notes, and scanned papers.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and many more. Select your language before extracting for best results.
