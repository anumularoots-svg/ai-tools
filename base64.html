Free Base64 encoder and decoder

ZapKitt Base64 tool converts text to Base64 encoding and decodes Base64 back to readable text. Base64 is commonly used in data URLs, email encoding (MIME), and embedding binary data in JSON or XML. All conversion happens in your browser — your data never leaves your device.
