How to resize images online for free

ZapKitt Image Resizer lets you change image dimensions instantly in your browser. Set exact pixel values, use percentage scaling, or pick from common presets like 1920×1080 for HD or 64×64 for icons. The aspect ratio lock ensures your images are never distorted.

All processing happens locally on your device. Unlike other image resizers that upload your photos to their servers, ZapKitt never sees your files. Perfect for resizing personal photos, product images, profile pictures, or thumbnails.

Does resizing reduce image quality?

Shrinking an image maintains quality well. Enlarging an image beyond its original dimensions can cause blurriness. For best results, start with a high-resolution image and resize downward.

Can I resize without changing aspect ratio?

Yes! The aspect ratio lock is on by default. When you change width, height adjusts automatically to maintain proportions. Click the lock icon to unlock and set custom dimensions independently.

What is the maximum image size?

ZapKitt can handle images up to 50MB. Since all processing happens in your browser, performance depends on your device. Very large images (8000×8000+) may take a few seconds on mobile devices.
