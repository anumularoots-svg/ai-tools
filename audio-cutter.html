Free online audio cutter and trimmer

ZapKitt Audio Cutter lets you trim any audio file directly in your browser. Load an MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG or FLAC file, drag the two handles across the waveform to mark the exact section you want, preview it, and download the result as a high-quality WAV. You can also remove a selected section instead of keeping it, and apply a smooth fade in and out. Everything runs locally on your device using the Web Audio API — your files are never uploaded to any server, so your audio stays private.
